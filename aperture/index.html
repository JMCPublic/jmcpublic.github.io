How to play

Fastest time winsFive objects. Everyone gets the same five each day, so you can compare times.
Six 20-second stagesEvery 20 seconds a free viewpoint opens in A and more of B fills in. Each object has 2 minutes.
Help adds 15 secondsA new viewpoint, fewer words (10 wrong ones go), or a vowel, which drops every copy of that vowel into your answer.
The wordsThe answer is somewhere in C's 56 words. Each word always has the same three-digit number, whatever round or day it appears in.
AnsweringTap words in C or type letters. It checks itself when every space is full. A wrong answer adds 15 seconds.
Your timeShown as total time with penalties in brackets, e.g. 2:30 (30). Not solved in 2 minutes counts as 2:00 plus 30 seconds.
