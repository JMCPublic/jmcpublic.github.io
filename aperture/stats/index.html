Each month starts fresh on the 1st (UK time) and is kept for good, so nothing ever needs deleting. Each year runs 1 November to 31 October. “New players” are devices opening the game for the first time that month. “All time” is the same running total shown in the Aperture games. Natural Wonders counts a visit once per game per session, and a game when its results appear; games played inside Masterbrain count as Masterbrain. Visits include your VIP visits; Premier access visits are kept out of everything else. Country comes from each device's time zone, so treat it as a close guide. Opening this page does not count as a visit. Each day starts fresh at midnight UK time and feeds into the month; daily numbers begin on 3 November 2026. Times and devices are recorded from 1 November 2026, so earlier visits don't appear in them.
