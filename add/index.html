Put the games on your home screen

You're inside the Two Minute Games app, the icon already on your home screen. Apps opened from an icon don't show Safari's address bar or Share button, so a new icon can't be added from here.
  1. Tap a game below to copy its address.
  2. Open the Safari app itself and paste the address into the address bar.
  3. Then follow the steps: ••• → Share → Add to Home Screen.

Each game gets its own icon and opens full screen, like an app. One tap and you're playing. Nothing to download.

  1. Open the game you want an icon for. Tap one below.
  2. In Safari's bar at the bottom, tap •••, then Share ⬆Older iPhones show Share straight away. Only see the web address? Scroll up a little and the full bar comes back. In Chrome, Share is at the top right.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add. That's it.

No address bar or Share button? You're probably inside a game you already put on your home screen: it opens as an app, without Safari's buttons. Open the Safari app itself, type the game's address (for example twominute.games/fictional), then follow the steps above.

Do it once for each game you want. Already added one and it shows an old picture? Remove it, refresh the page and add it again.
