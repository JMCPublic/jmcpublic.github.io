Slider info for nerds

Bounce (1–5): how much speed the ball keeps when it hits a wall. 1 is a dead thud (5% kept), 5 is lively (60% kept). Physicists call it the coefficient of restitution.

Speed (1–5): how hard the ball accelerates for a given tilt, and its top speed (about 190 to 490 pixels a second).

Tilt (8–40°): how far past your level you tip the phone before the ball is pulled at full strength. Low is twitchy, high is calm. Default 22°.

Steady (0–0.2): a dead zone around level. Tilts smaller than this share of the full range are ignored, so the ball sits still while you hold the phone roughly level. Default 0.06 (about 1.3°).

Uphill (0.4–1): how strongly the ball rolls away from you compared with towards you. 1 is equal; 0.75 means a quarter less pull uphill, which makes up for the way people tip the phone back.

Your level and these settings are saved on this phone and shared with the Find your angle page.
